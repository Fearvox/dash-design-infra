a website. who would have thought.

we've been on instagram for a long time and that's mostly where we live. but instagram is a stream, and streams don't hold up the longer pieces of writing we sometimes want to do — about a new drink, about a collab, about the day a regular brought their grandma in for the first time. the journal is for that. we'll post here every month or so. there's a subscribe form at the bottom of this page if email is easier than checking back.

a few things you'll see here over time:

- **the nectar lore.** people keep asking what's in it. we keep saying "a secret." we'll write a slightly longer "a secret" essay one of these months.
- **drops + collabs.** when something like gdspd x ppjzz comes around, here is where you'll find the long version.
- **just shop life.** the espresso machine's first big fight. the time we forgot to lock up. the saturday it rained and forty people came in anyway.

okay that's all for now. thanks for being here.

— godspeed
