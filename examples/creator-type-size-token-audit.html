Verdict
1Starter tokens exist where the PRs added them: --text-sm:12px and --text-xs:10px on the initial warm-paper/p5 surface set.
2This does not settle the full type scale. Display, h1, h2, label, and caption sizes remain contextual and should not be normalized mechanically.
3Family signature remains intact: warm-paper surfaces share an editorial zine hierarchy while dark-industrial and creative-hybrid surfaces retain their own role scales.
